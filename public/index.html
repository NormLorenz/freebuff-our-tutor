About Us

A husband-and-wife team who fell in love twice — first with each other, then with teaching German.

Anna Keller

👩‍🏫 Co-founder & Head Teacher

Born in Heidelberg, Anna taught German for 12 years before co-founding GutenTag. She believes grammar is best learned over cake and a good conversation. Ask her about the subjunctive — she'll light up like a Weihnachtsmarkt.

Fun fact: knows all lyrics to “99 Luftballons”… mostly correctly.

Lukas Keller

👨‍🏫 Co-founder & Learning Tech Lead

Lukas grew up in Dresden and spent a decade building learning software before teaming up with Anna. He designed our AI German tutor and is happiest when a student strings their first long, complicated — and correct — sentence together.

Fun fact: brews a legendary coffee for the coffee break in the Intermediate classes.
